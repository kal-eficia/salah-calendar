Open source · calculated in your browser

Put salah in your work calendar

Prayer times as calendar blocks, so meetings get booked around them, not over them. Download once, import into Google Calendar, Outlook or Apple Calendar.

Jumu'ah is blocked for an hour. Follow your local masjid where its timetable differs.

1. Download

One file with every prayer for the next 30 days, each as a busy block with a reminder 10 minutes before.

2. Import

Google Calendar: Settings, Import & export. Outlook: Add calendar, Upload from file. Apple Calendar: File, Import.

3. Protect

Colleagues see you're busy, so meetings land around salah. The command-line tool can also list meetings that already clash.

Source and the command-line tool: github.com/kal-eficia/salah-calendar · Built by Kalid Nur
